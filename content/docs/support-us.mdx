## Other ways to support the project

Financial sponsorship is not the only way to help:

- report reproducible problems through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/turboism/turboism/issues);
- propose focused improvements through [GitHub Pull Requests](https://github.com/turboism/turboism/pulls);
- test authorized Preview builds with dedicated test data and report the actual environment and results;
- improve English, Simplified Chinese, and Japanese documentation;
- develop independent Java plugins, GraalJS scripts, or MCP integrations within the public boundaries; and
- help other community members through [Discord](https://discord.gg/bect4anknH).

For sponsorship questions or to discuss another form of support, email [contact@turboism.dev](mailto:contact@turboism.dev).

## Independence

Support does not make Turboism an official Live2D or Cubism product and does not transfer rights belonging to their respective owners. Read [Safety and Rights](./safety-and-rights) before presenting a sponsorship, partnership, or endorsement involving the project.
